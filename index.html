What is Nabad?
Nabad is a credit-based membership that gives you access to hundreds of studios, gyms, and wellness experiences across Lebanon.
How do credits work?
Each plan comes with credits to use for booking classes. The credit value of a reservation varies by location, popularity, and time.
What's the benefit of booking on Nabad?
Nabad gives you an affordable way to explore studios, gyms, and wellness spaces at discounted rates — all under one membership.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Pause or cancel your plan in the app, anytime. No contracts, no fees, no friction.
