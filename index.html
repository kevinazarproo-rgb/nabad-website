FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which studios are part of Nabad?
All partner studios are listed in the app. You can browse the full network by city, activity, or neighborhood and pick what fits your week.
Can I use Nabad in different cities?
Yes. One pass works at every Nabad studio — Beirut, Jounieh, Tripoli, Batroun, and growing.
Do credits roll over if I don't use them?
Unused credits roll over for 30 days on monthly plans, so a slow week is never wasted.
Can I pause or cancel?
Pause or cancel any time from the app — no fees, no friction.
I own a studio — how do I join?
