How to refer a friend to Nabad

On the app: open the Nabad app, tap the Profile tab in the lower right corner, and select Refer a friend.

On the website: log into your account, click your initial in the top right corner, navigate to Account, and click Refer friends.

How to invite a friend to class

Search for any class on Nabad and tap the Invite button, or use the invite link that appears once you reserve a spot.

Already booked but forgot to invite a friend? Tap Upcoming in the app to view your future classes and invite friends in one tap.
