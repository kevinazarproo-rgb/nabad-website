Founding Members

Bring your friends.
Become a founding member.

Nabad launches with the people who built it. Share Nabad with friends, earn a badge, and climb the Founding Member leaderboard.

Share on WhatsApp

How it works

Step 01
Share Nabad
Send your friends a link via WhatsApp or copy/paste it anywhere. No code, no signup yet — it's pre-launch.
Step 02
They join the list
When friends sign up with your link, they're tied to your account and counted toward your Founding Member status.
Step 03
Earn a badge
The more friends, the higher your tier. Founding Members get a visible badge in the app and a spot on the public leaderboard at launch.

Founding Member tiers

Every successful invite counts. The higher you go, the louder your status at launch.

Quick questions

Is there a money or credit reward?
Not yet — we keep prices low for everyone instead. Founding Members get badges, early access, and public recognition at launch.
When does my count start?
As soon as a friend joins the waitlist with your shared link, they're tied to your account. We'll surface your count in the app at launch.
Can I lose my badge?
No. Founding Member status is permanent — once you hit Gold, you stay Gold forever.
Where can I see my friends?
In the Nabad app at launch, you'll see who joined with your link and where you rank on the public leaderboard.
