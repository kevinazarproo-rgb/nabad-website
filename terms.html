Last updated: May 2026

1. Acceptance of terms

By creating a Nabad account or using our services, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use Nabad.

2. Your membership

Nabad is a monthly credit-based membership that gives you access to partner studios in Lebanon. Your plan renews automatically each month until you cancel from the app.

  • You must be at least 16 years old to sign up.
  • You are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure.
  • Memberships are personal and non-transferable.

3. Credits

Each plan includes a monthly credit allowance. Credit costs per class are shown in the app before booking and vary by studio, time, and demand. Unused credits roll over for 30 days on monthly plans.

4. Bookings, cancellations, and no-shows

Cancel at least 12 hours before class to recover your credits. Late cancellations and no-shows forfeit the credits used and may incur a fee set by the partner studio.

5. Pausing & cancellation

You can pause or cancel your plan anytime from the app. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. We do not refund partial months.

You agree to follow each partner studio's house rules, arrive on time, and treat staff and other members with respect. We reserve the right to suspend accounts that abuse the service or harm partner studios.
